## 2. Mô tả nội dung môn học (Course Description)

GRC490 is an integrative capstone experience bridging Business Administration and Software Engineering. Interdisciplinary teams identify a genuine market problem, validate the business opportunity, design and build a functioning, deployable digital product, and defend both its business viability and technical implementation. Each team is guided by two co-supervisors—one from each faculty—and each student is assessed individually before a mixed committee.

## 3. Mục tiêu học tập cốt lõi (Key Learning Outcomes)

- **CLO Name**: CLO Details
- **CLO1**: Demonstrate an ability to integrate cross-disciplinary knowledge (business and technology), handle complexity, and plan and carry out advanced tasks independently and as a group member within specified time limits.
- **CLO2**: Use appropriate market research methodology to identify market problems, validate demand, and propose a digital solution that is both technically feasible and commercially viable.
- **CLO3**: Design, implement, and test a software system aligned with stated business requirements, using sound software engineering principles: requirements, architecture, iterative development, and testing.
- **CLO4**: Develop critical thinking, creative problem-solving, and management capabilities; analyze marketing, operational, and financial aspects of the digital product in theory and practice.
- **CLO5**: Apply project-management principles to coordinate an interdisciplinary team and deliver a working digital product within schedule and quality constraints.
- **CLO6**: Write, present and defend the thesis—including the business case and technical design—according to well-defined academic and professional criteria, with correct referencing.
- **CLO7**: Produce a feasible, deployable digital product or near-production MVP that demonstrates real-world entrepreneurial and engineering value and supports actual or potential business operation.
