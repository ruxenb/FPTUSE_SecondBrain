## 2. Mô tả nội dung môn học (Course Description)

The IT Interdisciplinary Graduation Project is an integrative capstone experience that consolidates the knowledge, skills, and competencies acquired throughout the undergraduate into a practical, research-oriented project. This course challenges students from the disciplines of IT to collaboratively apply their analytical, problem-solving, and technical expertise to address real-world problems. By working across disciplinary boundaries, student teams are expected to design, implement, and evaluate solutions that reflect the convergence of modern IT fields. This course is structured for teams of 3–6 members, drawn from any combination of the IT specializations (IT, IA, SE, AI,...), thereby fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and teamwork. Individual enrollment is permitted under specific conditions approved by the supervising lecturer and head of department/faculty. All projects must incorporate a substantive research component, regardless of specialization. This includes:     - Problem Definition: A clear articulation of the research problem and its significance within the IT domain     - Literature Review: A critical synthesis of relevant prior work, identifying gaps that the project aims to address     - Proposed Solution or Model: A well-justified technical approach (e.g., an AI/ML model, a security mechanism, a software system, or a hybrid combination thereof)     - Experimental Evaluation: Rigorous testing and empirical analysis to validate the proposed solution, with quantitative and/or qualitative results     - Discussion, Conclusion, and Future Work: Interpretation of findings and directions for further research

## 3. Mục tiêu học tập cốt lõi (Key Learning Outcomes)

- **CLO Name**: CLO Details
- **CLO1**: Identify, define, and scope a meaningful research or engineering problem within the IT domain
- **CLO2**: Conduct a systematic literature review and critically evaluate existing knowledge and approaches
- **CLO3**: Design and implement an integrated IT solution that draws on principles from software engineering, artificial intelligence, and/or information security as appropriate to the project context
- **CLO4**: Apply the full software development lifecycle, from requirements and design through implementation, testing, and deployment, with appropriate quality assurance practices
- **CLO5**: Evaluate proposed solutions through rigorous experimental methods and interpret results objectively
- **CLO6**: Communicate research findings and technical decisions effectively through formal written documentation and an oral presentation before an examination board
